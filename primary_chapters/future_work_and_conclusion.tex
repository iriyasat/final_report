\chapter{Future Work \& Conclusion}

\section{Future Works}
Although the automotive dealership management platform successfully fulfills all core functional, technical, and analytical requirements established for the internship project, several future enhancements can further elevate its enterprise capabilities. As transaction volumes and e-commerce traffic scale nationwide, transitioning the backend from a monolithic Django application toward containerized microservices—orchestrated via Kubernetes and gRPC communication—will improve fault isolation and independent service scaling. In parallel, developing a dedicated cross-platform mobile application using the Flutter framework will empower field sales executives to scan VIN barcodes, record vehicle sales, and respond to buyer inquiries directly from mobile devices.

Real-time communication within the platform can be further modernized by replacing client-side HTTP polling with WebSockets or Server-Sent Events via Django Channels, delivering instant push notifications to sales staff whenever new holding orders or test-drive appointments are submitted. Furthermore, integrating machine learning regression models trained on historical sales transactions and regional economic indicators will automate market price valuations and predictive inventory demand forecasting. Finally, incorporating commercial online payment gateways, such as SSLCommerz, bKash, or Stripe, will enable retail customers to submit vehicle reservation deposits and complete digital checkout workflows securely online.

\section{Conclusion}
During my 3-month undergraduate internship at \textbf{Radiant Pharmaceuticals Limited} (ICT Wing, MIS) from May 17, 2026, to August 16, 2026, I successfully engineered, optimized, benchmarked, and documented an integrated enterprise automotive management platform. The resulting software unifies internal dealership ERP workflows (\texttt{car\_sales}) with a customer-centric web storefront (\texttt{ecommerce}) within a single high-performance Django framework.

The platform addresses critical operational bottlenecks by coupling a high-performance star-schema analytical model with a robust nine-tier role-based access control matrix, custom numeric identifier authentication, and atomic database transaction handling. By deploying hand-crafted raw SQL aggregation routines and composite foreign key indexing, the system delivers near-instantaneous reporting dashboards alongside an asynchronous, responsive public catalog featuring dynamic filtering, pricing benchmarks, and direct showroom inquiry routing.

This project fully satisfies the academic requirements of the CSE499 Senior Project / Internship course under the Outcome-Based Education (OBE) curriculum at \textbf{Independent University, Bangladesh (IUB)}. The practical experience acquired in full-stack backend engineering, relational database optimization, transactional security, and corporate ICT collaboration at Radiant Pharmaceuticals Limited has provided an invaluable foundation for my professional career in software engineering.
